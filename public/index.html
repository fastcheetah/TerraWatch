TRANSPARENT SCIENCE

Know what you're looking at.

Data points represent potential active-fire detections with time, coordinates, satellite, confidence, temperature, and brightness. The map may show a capped set of the most recent matching points, while charts are computed from all records matching your filters. Data in demo mode is synthetic. For scientific interpretation, identify the original sensor, units, processing and quality controls.
